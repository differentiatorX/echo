What the edit does

Every clip leaves finished.

Cold open

The clip's strongest line plays first as a teaser, then a hard cut back to the start.

Jump cuts, punch-ins

Silences and filler words go. Framing shifts every few seconds so the eye never rests.

Captions people read

Huge word-by-word captions, highlighted keywords, an on-screen hook, a progress bar and your watermark.

Speaker reframing

Face-centered vertical crops, automatic split screen for two-person podcasts, blurred backdrop for screen recordings.

Edit by words

Click words to cut them, rewrite the hook, change caption style, re-render one clip in seconds.

Trial reels

Optionally post to non-followers first. Instagram shares the winners with your followers on its own.

Why it's yours
